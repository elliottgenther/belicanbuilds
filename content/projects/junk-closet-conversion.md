The guest room closet had become the household's catch-all — wire racks loaded with bags, boxes, and the accumulated evidence of a busy life with a toddler. A good friend's wife needed somewhere to think, work, and close a door behind her. This was the space.

We demoed the wire rack system, patched the walls, and painted the interior fresh. Then we cut a full-width, full-depth desktop from birch plywood — a proper surface, not a shelf pretending to be one. Minimal floating shelves on each side wall keep books and personal items off the desk without cluttering the space. A high shelf and a black steel clothing bar up top mean the room still functions for guests when needed.

The result is more than a nook. It's a reminder that the chaos of raising a toddler is a phase — and that even in the middle of it, there's always room to carve out a little space for yourself.
