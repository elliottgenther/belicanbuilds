## Before

This was a big back yard with nowhere to go.

![Stage before](/images/projects/stage/stage-1.jpeg)

## After

We created a large stage topped with a clear roof to preserve the view of the trees and sky. We also added hardware to hang a projector screen for outdoor movies. However, the standout feature of this stage is for live performances, which on many occasions have brought the neighborhood together for lively evenings full of music.

A simple firepit was added to anchor the space and keep folks warm on chilly nights, and an owl box was added high in the trees above the stage to attract Barred Owls that frequent the space.

The unruly azalea bushes were also thinned out considerably, opening the space further, and some time later, the existing chain link fence was replaced with a 6ft wooden one to bring some privacy back to the space.
