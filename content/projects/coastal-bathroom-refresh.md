## Before

The existing bathroom felt dark and dated, with old tile, a small vanity, and limited storage. The fixtures were mismatched and the room did not reflect the home's coastal setting.

## After

We refreshed the space with crisp white tile, a floating wood vanity, and a touch of texture in the shower niche. New lighting and a refined color palette make the room feel bright, calm, and intentional.

## The Story

This refresh was all about making every inch count. We improved storage with built-in shelves, swapped the tub for a glass-walled shower, and chose finishes that are both timeless and durable for a high-humidity environment.
