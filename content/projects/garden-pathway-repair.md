## Before

The garden path was uneven, overgrown, and difficult to navigate after rain. The old stones had shifted, making it hard to carry tools or move safely through the space.

## After

We repaired and reset the path using new materials and a low-maintenance edging system. The finished route is level, easy to sweep, and visually tied to the planting beds around it.

## The Story

The goal was not just to fix the path, but to make it feel like part of the landscape. We chose materials that complement the yard and built a route that supports gardening routines instead of interrupting them.
