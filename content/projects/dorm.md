This was a simple, straightforward job. A mom reached out before her daughter's freshman year at Clemson and asked for two things.

## The Build

First, a better desk. The one that comes standard in the dorm room is small. I built a larger desktop to sit over it, with a small bookshelf underneath and a hutch on top for storage.

Second, a pantry. A small standalone unit that fits over a mini fridge, with food storage on either side.

## Move-In

What caught me by surprise was how much this build sent me back to my own freshman dorm room at the Kelly House in Charleston. I remember how excited I was to bring a little bit of home with me and set up my workspace. I remember that room, and the people I shared it with. More than 20 years later, they're still good friends.

I hope this desk sees some late nights and good company.
