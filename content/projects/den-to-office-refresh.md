## Before

This den was oozing with 70s nostalgia, but past its prime and in need of a fresh start and a new use case.

![Den before 1](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-1.jpeg)

![Den before 2](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-2.png)

## After

A big minimalist office that is crisp, offers a large open workspace, and is ready for a new theme to carry it into the foreseeable future.

![Office after](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-3.jpeg)

## Details

- Removed popcorn ceilings.
- Borrowed closet space from an adjacent room and added a built-in bookshelf and cabinet for storage.
- Prepped, primed, and painted the ceiling, walls, and trim.
