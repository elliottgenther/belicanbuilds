The den had good bones but it was tired. Popcorn ceiling, dark trim, a layout that did not have a clear purpose anymore. I needed a home office. This room was going to become one.

## Before

![Den before](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-1.jpeg)

![Den before, second view](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-2edit.jpeg)

## After

I scraped the popcorn ceiling, repaired the drywall, and painted everything fresh. Then I borrowed some square footage from an adjacent closet and built a bookshelf and cabinet into the wall. It gave the room storage without taking up floor space.

The result is a big, open workspace. Nothing competing for your attention. Just room to work.

![Office after](/images/projects/den-to-office-refresh/den-to-office-3.jpeg)

## Details

- Removed popcorn ceiling and repaired drywall
- Borrowed adjacent closet space for built-in bookshelf and cabinet
- Prepped, primed, and painted ceiling, walls, and trim
