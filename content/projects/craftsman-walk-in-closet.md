## Before

The existing closet was a cramped reach-in with low shelving and wasted vertical space. Clothes were stacked, and there was no easy place for shoes, linens, or daily outfits.

## After

We turned the space into a walk-in closet with a craftsman feel: built-in drawers, open shelving, and a dedicated dressing nook. Rich wood tones and matte black hardware make it feel more like a room than a closet.

## The Story

We framed the closet with a borrowed light and redesigned the interior layout for easy access. Each shelf and drawer was sized for the client's wardrobe, and the new layout makes getting ready faster and more enjoyable.
