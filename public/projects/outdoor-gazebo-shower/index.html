Outdoor Gazebo Shower
Projects

Elliott Genther

May 10, 2026

Problem

There was no inherent problem that this project addressed. The outdoor shower was simply a desired amenity. It did, however, take an empty and neglected space in the client’s back yard and made it a beautiful destination with a purpose.

Before

Intuition guided the footprint of the structure. We wanted it to make use of the full space without overcrowding it. The structure felt just right 3ft off the side of the sunroom and the back of the house. Enough space to accommodate a lawn mower.

After

The final design came together really nicely, with the back wall, built in cubby, and all the trim finishing the space in aromatic cedar. We also added trellis on each side of the structure and planted jasmine. In a few years it should cover it entirely. Finished everything off with a coat of pinifin oil.
