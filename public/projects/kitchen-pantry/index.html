Kitchen Pantry
Now We're Cookin'

Elliott Genther

September 2026

Before this pantry went in, it had the wire rack shelving you find in a lot of new builds around here. Cookie cutter. An afterthought. And when no intention goes into a build, it shows in how the space gets used. Things got shoved wherever they fit, and cooking became something to get through instead of something to enjoy.

The customer wanted something stylish, but most of all, functional.

Three big drawers at the bottom hide the bulky stuff. Tall slots on the right get the pans out from under the stove and into a line of sight.

The top shelf is recessed on purpose, so you can actually see and reach what’s back there. Skip that step and the can of beans in the back becomes a 32-year-old that doesn’t pay rent.

A pantry that looks good, stays organized, and shows you what’s on hand works on a whole other level. It makes you want to cook instead of reaching for a frozen pizza.

Details

  • Replaced builder-grade wire rack shelving with a custom pantry
  • Built three deep drawers to hide bulky items
  • Added tall vertical slots for pans, moved out from under the stove
  • Recessed the top shelf for visibility and easy reach
